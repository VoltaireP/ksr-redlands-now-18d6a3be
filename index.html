This week at a glance

  • How things are going. 290 people signed up for the newsletter this week at $0.83 each, on $240.72 of spend compared with $222.74 the week before. Reach rose to 12,322 from 11,430 and clicks to 431 from 416, while landing page visits held at 379.
  • What to look into. A page-promotion campaign from a year ago is still spending a little each week and brought no sign-ups, and the separate reach campaign brought none either, by design. We recommend switching the old page-promotion campaign off and letting the sign-up campaign carry that budget.
  • What is next. Next we will keep the sign-up campaign as the main spend and recommend moving the small reach and page-promotion budgets into it, since every sign-up this week came from the sign-up campaign. We will also give the weekend planner ad a real budget.
  • Creative ideas to consider. Every top ad tells one growth story. The December image, 122 sign-ups on $73.01, opens 'RedlandsNow didn't launch. It detonated,' says the newsletter grew over 400 percent with real local stories, and says hop in while the rocket is climbing. The January image, 'Something big is brewing in town,' did nearly as well. The weekend planner, 'What should we do this weekend in the Redlands area?', got 4 sign-ups on $1.95. Next we will fund that angle, since it sells a weekly use, not growth.
