This week at a glance

  • How things are going. Meta counted 256 sign-ups at $0.93 each, against 273 at $0.89 the week before, on $237.21 of spend. CRM leads are not available because no subscriber list is connected.
  • What to look into. Reach keeps shrinking: 6,033 people this week against 6,541 the week before and 10,087 the week before that, on nearly the same spend. The ads are reaching fewer new people.
  • What is next. We recommend testing one new image with a fresh opening line, since the top five ads share three openings and reach is falling.
  • Creative ideas to consider. The 'RedlandsNow didn't launch. It detonated' copy, which says the newsletter grew more than 400%, ran on three top ads: 51 sign-ups at $0.89, 46 at $0.86 and 38 at $1.19. 'Something big is brewing in town' brought 45 at $0.96. The weekend plans post, 'Never ask this question again,' brought 44 at $0.48, the cheapest. Worth testing more weekend plans ads, such as an image of this week's events, since that angle brings the cheapest sign-ups.
