This week at a glance

  • How things are going. Meta counted 259 newsletter sign-ups at $0.90 each, down from 290 at $0.83 the week before. Ad spend was $234.38, the ads reached 11,565 people, and 342 people landed on the sign-up page, down from 379.
  • What to look into. Reach, link clicks, landing page views and sign-ups all slipped a little from the week before on almost the same spend. A page follow promotion dated 09/30/2025 is still running: it spent $13.47 and reached 993 people. Worth confirming it is still wanted.
  • What is next. We recommend keeping the December refresh ad as the main ad and giving the weekend planner ad a larger share of budget for one week, to see if its low cost per sign-up holds.
  • Creative ideas to consider. The December refresh image ad brought 122 sign-ups at $0.61. It opens 'RedlandsNow didn't launch. It detonated,' and says the newsletter grew more than 400% since September. The same copy on the July control ad brought 49 at $1.12, and on the August refresh image 41 at $0.88. The weekend planner ad, 'What should we do this weekend in the Redlands area?', brought 8 on $1.30 of spend, too little to judge. We recommend testing it with more budget, since it sells a clear weekly benefit.
