This week at a glance

  • How things are going. Meta counted 245 newsletter sign-ups at $0.98 each, down from 285 at $0.84 the week before. Ad spend held at $240.38, while link clicks rose to 657 from 435 and page views to 485 from 383.
  • What to look into. More people reached the sign-up page but fewer signed up. The new Winner Iterations image ad drew 252 link clicks and only 17 sign-ups, so its clicks are likely not turning into sign-ups yet. The 09/30/2025 page promotion also spent $14.69.
  • What is next. We recommend giving the new Winner Iterations ad a few more days, then comparing its cost per sign-up with the December image ad before moving more budget to it.
  • Creative ideas to consider. The December image ad brought 90 sign-ups at $0.77. It opens 'RedlandsNow didn't launch. It detonated,' and says the newsletter grew more than 400% since September. The weekend plans post ad brought 71 at $0.85 with 'What should we do this weekend in the Redlands area? Never ask this question again.' The January image ad, 'Something big is brewing in town,' brought 36 at $0.86. We recommend testing a weekend plans image ad, since it is the only top idea that says what a reader gets each week.
